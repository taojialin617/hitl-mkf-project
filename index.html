From fragmented maintenance records to connected diagnostic knowledge.

HitL-MKF combines LLM voting and expert review with entity alignment and path fusion. The resulting knowledge graph supports Two-Stage GraphRAG for CNC fault diagnosis.

Method overview

Human review, where it matters.
  1. 01

    Extract

    Structure work orders into Alarm, Phenomenon, Reason and Solution.

  2. 02

    Review & fuse

    Use HitL-Vote and expert review to align entities and fuse compatible paths.

  3. 03

    Retrieve & reason

    Construct an explicit reasoning path, then generate maintenance suggestions.
