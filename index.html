01 / THE METHOD

Better knowledge.
Before the answer.

Expert adjudication at the points of uncertainty. Fusion at two levels. Explicit reasoning paths before maintenance suggestions.

02 / THE EVIDENCE

Consolidation matters.

Compare the graph construction stages under the same Two-Stage GraphRAG strategy, evaluated on 209 held-out questions.

BERT-F1

+0.0762 BERT-F1 over Raw KG under the same two-stage strategy.

Read the results in context. Evaluated on data from a single CNC manufacturer. Improvements are descriptive; no statistical significance or real-time guarantees are claimed.
